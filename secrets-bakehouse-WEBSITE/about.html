A new name. The same proper baking.

Formerly Pontnewydd Bakery and Cornerstone Bakery, we’re now Secrets Bakehouse, with four shops across Gwent and a team of more than 30 people.

Who we are

Local bakers, local favourites

For years, locals in Pontnewydd have popped into the bakery on Chapel Street for warm pasties, pies and cakes, and Newport regulars have done the same at Cornerstone on Alexandra Road. Today both are proudly Secrets Bakehouse.

Since the new name went up, we’ve doubled in size: four shops across Gwent, including Cefn Fforest and Fleur-de-lis, a team of more than 30 bakers and shop staff, and delivery through Just Eat and Uber Eats. Some days in Newport the pasties are gone by 11:30am, and honestly, that’s our favourite kind of problem.
