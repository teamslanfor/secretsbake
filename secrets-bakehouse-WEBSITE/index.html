So what’s the secret?

Honestly? There isn’t one. Just proper baking.

You might know us as Pontnewydd Bakery in Cwmbran or Cornerstone Bakery in Newport. The name on the sign has changed, but the baking hasn’t: traditional recipes, made by hand, fresh every single day.

  1. Bake everything fresh on site, every morning.
  2. Stick to recipes that have stood the test of time.
  3. Make it generous. (Have you seen the size of our pasties?)
  4. Serve it with a proper warm Welsh welcome.
