Privacy & cookies

This policy explains how Secrets Bakehouse Ltd (“we”), 42 Alexandra Road, Newport NP20 2JE, uses personal information collected through this website.

What we collect

When you send an enquiry, cake request or afternoon tea pre-order, we collect the details you give us, such as your name, phone number, email address, collection date, chosen shop and any photo you attach.

How we use it

We use your details only to reply to you and arrange your order. We never sell your information or use it for marketing without your permission.

How forms are delivered

Website forms are delivered to our email using the FormSubmit service. Delivery orders are handled by Just Eat and Uber Eats, and Facebook messages by Meta, under their own privacy policies.

Cookies

This website doesn’t use tracking or advertising cookies. The embedded Google Maps and Google Fonts may set their own cookies or log technical data. If you dismiss the cookie notice, your browser remembers that choice locally.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your information, or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing secretsbakehouse@hotmail.com. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).
